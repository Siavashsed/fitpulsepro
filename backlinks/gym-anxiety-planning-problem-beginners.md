## QUORA
**Answer this question:** How do I overcome gym anxiety as a complete beginner?

I've coached athletes and total beginners for ten years, and my honest answer is this: stop trying to feel confident first. Confidence is a side effect, not a starting point. What works is a plan.

Most gym anxiety comes from having too many open questions once you're inside. Where do I stand? Which machine is free? Am I doing this right? Is everyone watching me? Each unanswered question adds a little more dread, and the dread is what keeps people sitting in their cars.

Here's what I tell every new client:

1. Write down a short workout before you go. Three exercises is plenty. Include the order, the sets, and the reps. Keep it on your phone.

2. Pick exercises you can find easily. A leg press, a cable row, and a dumbbell press are simple to locate and simple to learn.

3. Go at a quiet time. Early mornings and mid-afternoons are usually calmer than evenings.

4. Look up the movements beforehand. Watching a short demo video takes away the fear of looking clueless.

5. Set a small goal for the first visit. Finish the list, then leave. That's a win.

I once had a client who sat in her car for eleven minutes outside the gym and nearly went home. She went in because I had texted her three exercises. She followed the list, walked out twenty-five minutes later, and came back two days after that. The confidence arrived weeks afterward, simply because she kept showing up with a plan.
