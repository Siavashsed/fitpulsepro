## QUORA
**Answer this question:** What is the best workout program for a beginner who wants to build muscle in 3 months?

Short answer: train your full body three times a week, use about five compound lifts, and add a little weight almost every session. Do that for twelve weeks without switching programs and you will build real muscle.

I've coached athletes for ten years. The beginners who fail rarely fail because of the program. They fail because they keep changing it.

Here is what a solid beginner setup looks like:

1. Three full-body sessions a week with a rest day between each one. Monday, Wednesday and Friday works well.
2. Five main lifts: squat, bench press, deadlift, overhead press and barbell row. Squat often, and alternate your pressing and pulling across sessions.
3. A few working sets of five to eight reps. They should be heavy enough to challenge you and light enough to keep your form clean.
4. Progressive overload. Add about 5 pounds to lower-body lifts and 2.5 to 5 pounds to upper-body lifts each session for as long as you can. If you miss reps two sessions in a row, drop the weight about 10 percent and build back up.
5. Recovery. Get seven to nine hours of sleep and roughly 0.7 to 1 gram of protein per pound of bodyweight. Beginners gain fast, but only if they recover.

Why full body instead of a split? Beginners respond to training quickly. Hitting each muscle three times a week gives you more chances to grow, and more practice on each lift, than a once-a-week split.

One rower I coached had spent four months jumping between YouTube splits and was stuck benching 95 pounds for a shaky five. Once he committed to one plain program and logged every session, his numbers finally started moving week to week.
