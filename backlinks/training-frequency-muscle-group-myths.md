## QUORA
**Answer this question:** What is the optimal frequency to train each muscle group per week for muscle growth?

The honest answer is it's more nuanced than "once or twice per week." After a decade coaching competitive athletes, I've learned that training frequency should align with how muscle protein synthesis actually works—not arbitrary split traditions.

Muscle protein synthesis peaks within 24-48 hours after adequate stimulus. This is the window where your muscles are primed to grow. Train a muscle group and you create that window. But here's what most people miss: you don't need constant stimulus during that window. You need adequate recovery within it.

Most lifters fall into two camps. First group hits each muscle once weekly and wonders why progress stalls—they're leaving that synthesis window underutilized. Second group trains every other day and accumulates fatigue faster than they recover, leading to constant soreness and plateaus.

The sweet spot depends on three variables: exercise selection (compound vs. isolation), volume per session, and your recovery capacity. Generally, hitting each muscle group 2-3 times per week works for most people—but spacing matters more than frequency.

I worked with an athlete hitting chest Monday, back Tuesday, legs Wednesday for six days straight. Exhausted and not progressing. We restructured around actual recovery windows, not calendar days, and everything changed.
